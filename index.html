Colmar Fairytale Day: Real-Life Beauty and the Beast Village
• Morning (08:45 – 12:45): Board 30 min SNCF TER Fluo train from Strasbourg to Colmar (**08:45 AM – 09:25 AM**). Step into the preserved medieval old town (Vieille Ville), the primary real-life visual inspiration for Belle's 'Poor Provincial Town' in Disney's Beauty and the Beast. Photograph La Maison des Têtes (1609 Renaissance facade with 106 grotesque sculpted heads). Admire Maison Pfister, Colmar's iconic 1537 wooden gallery residence, two-story corner oriel turret, and painted frescoes featured in Disney's concept sketches. Walk down Rue des Marchands, the cobblestone lane Belle walks through singing 'Little town, it's a quiet village...'. Tour Collégiale Saint-Martin de Colmar with green/yellow glazed Alsatian roof tiles.
• Afternoon (13:00 – 17:30): Photograph Fontaine Schwendi at Place de l'Ancienne Douane (Koïfhus square) — the exact stone fountain Belle sits on while reading her sheep/book story! Sample local Munster cheeses, kougelhopf, and warm baguettes inside the 1865 Marché Couvert ('There goes the baker with his tray, like always'). Stroll Quai de la Poissonnerie, the pastel half-timbered fisherman's row reflecting in the water. Explore La Petite Venise where the Lauch River branches beneath flower-lined timber footbridges.
• Evening (18:00 – 20:30): Return TER train to Strasbourg/Kehl; festive Christmas Eve dinner at hotel base. Prepare backpacks for early 03:00 AM checkout and dawn coach into the Swiss Alps.
