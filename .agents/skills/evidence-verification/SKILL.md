# Evidence-First Verification & Fact-Checking Standard

This skill establishes the mandatory verification protocol for all physiological, nutritional, biomechanical, and data-analytical responses.

## Core Directives

### 1. Mandatory Pre-Response Verification Checklist
Before presenting any conclusion, calculation, or analysis:
- [ ] **Math & Data Audit:** Verify all arithmetic, percentage calculations, date deltas, and spreadsheet/app values against original sources.
- [ ] **Empirical Separation:** Separate raw recorded data (e.g. scale weight, tape circumference, BIA impedance, app logs) from inferences or coaching interpretations.
- [ ] **Zero Unverified Speculation:** Never make declarative statements about an individual's private intent, psychology, or unrecorded behaviors (e.g., assuming a high-calorie day was an "uncontrolled binge" rather than a planned refeed).

### 2. Grounding Claims in Peer-Reviewed Science
Whenever explaining physiological or nutritional mechanisms, anchor claims in consensus exercise science and peer-reviewed literature:
- **Caloric Deficits & Protein Sparing:** Reference landmark systematic reviews (e.g., Helms et al., Phillips & Van Loon, Morton et al., ISSN position stands).
- **Carbohydrate & Glycogen Dynamics:** Reference metabolic ward investigations on de novo lipogenesis (DNL), GLUT-4 translocation, and non-oxidative glucose disposal (e.g., Acheson et al., McDevitt et al., Jensen et al.).
- **Creatine Osmolality & ATP-PCr Resynthesis:** Reference established literature on intracellular water expansion and ergogenic outcomes (e.g., Kreider et al., Hultman et al., Antonio et al.).
- **Food Composition & Nutrient Densities:** Ground all raw and cooked macro values in the USDA FoodData Central database.

### 3. Clickable Citation Standards
Always format citations as clickable Markdown links pointing directly to PubMed, DOI, or official institutional databases:
- Systematic Reviews / Meta-analyses: `[Author et al. Year (Journal)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/PMID/)`
- Clinical Studies: `[Author et al. Year (Journal)](https://doi.org/DOI)`
- Official Nutritional Data: `[USDA FoodData Central FDC ID XXXXXX](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-app.html#/food-details/XXXXXX/nutrients)`

### 4. Tri-Tier Epistemic Transparency
Every statement must fall cleanly into one of three tiers:
1. **Tier 1: Verified Direct Data:** Explicitly confirmed numbers from client logs, scales, BIA sheets, or direct measurements.
2. **Tier 2: Peer-Reviewed Scientific Consensus:** Established biological facts supported by peer-reviewed evidence (with citations and links).
3. **Tier 3: Coaching Interpretation / Working Hypothesis:** Inferences, trend forecasts, or tactical adjustments, explicitly labeled as *"Working Hypothesis"* or *"Coaching Interpretation"*.
